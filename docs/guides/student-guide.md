# Student Guide

PRISM gives students a private view of assessment results that a teacher has explicitly released.

## First Sign-In

1. Open the deployment's `/login` page.
2. Enter the email address and temporary password provided by your teacher.
3. Create a new password when prompted.
4. Sign-in continues with the new password and opens the student portal.

The new password must contain at least 12 characters. Changing it revokes older sessions and creates a fresh session in the current browser.

If access is disabled or credentials need to be reset, contact the teacher who manages the class. There is no public account-registration or password-reset email flow.

## Released Results

The **Results** area lists only papers released by a teacher. Select a paper to inspect:

- exam title and date
- total marks and maximum marks
- criterion-level marks
- the reason recorded for each decision
- review recommendation labels where present

A paper that is still processing, under teacher review, unreleased, withdrawn, archived, or assigned to another student is not available in the student portal.

## Learning Profile

The profile summarizes concepts represented in released submissions. Percentages and mastery bands are calculated by the backend from effective criterion marks. Effective marks use a teacher override when one exists; otherwise they use the AI suggestion.

The profile may identify stronger and developing concepts and link those observations to assessment evidence. It does not infer intelligence, personality, motivation, mental health, cheating, honesty, or other personal traits.

## Data And Corrections

The student portal is read-only. Ask the teacher about a possible marking or account issue. Teachers can inspect original page evidence, request an AI re-evaluation, apply an override, and release the resulting decision.

Do not share account credentials or leave a session open on a shared device. Use **Sign out** when finished.
